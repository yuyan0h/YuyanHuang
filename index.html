Selected Projects

01 C-SmartGPT Aug 2024 – Dec 2025
Multi-agent planning support for urban carbon governance · Master’s thesis, SuSmart City Lab
  • Built a vector-based RAG pipeline over a heterogeneous corpus of carbon inventories, socio-economic statistics, policy texts and literature — 92.5% retrieval accuracy on an internal benchmark.
  • Constructed and visualised a 100,000+ triple knowledge graph, combining top-down ontology design with bottom-up GraphRAG extraction.
  • Designed a four-agent orchestration layer (retrieval → reasoning → drafting → verification) producing structured city-level carbon diagnostic and mitigation reports.
Python · LLM APIs · agent orchestration · vector RAG · graph store · GraphRAG
02 TAMPUS Jul 2025
Deep reinforcement learning for transit route optimisation · Project lead · Advisors: Prof. Zhong-Ren Peng (U. Florida), Dr. Dongjie Wang (U. Kansas)
  • Implemented deep RL models on origin–destination data to optimise bus routes and scheduling in Tampa, Florida.
  • Led front-end development of a web platform with integrated LLM APIs, letting planners interrogate and act on optimisation results in natural language.
PyTorch · Deep RL · OD data processing · HTML/JS · LLM API integration

Multi-scale carbon visualisation platform · Product manager & UI/UX designer, SuSmart City Lab
  • Defined product requirements and natural-language interaction features for a carbon and pollution platform serving policymakers and the public.

Product design · HTML/JS · multi-scale geospatial visualisation · Figma
